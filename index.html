M.R. Systems & Technology is a privately held company based in Lakewood, New Jersey. For a decade, it has built and run the systems behind working businesses, including its own.

The company is led by its founder, Mordechai (Mordy) Rothschild. Its approach is simple: stay close to the work, keep things clear, and treat people well.
